# NovaRetail Refund Policy

## Standard Refunds

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original delivery date.

## Damaged Products

If a product arrives damaged, the customer should contact NovaRetail Customer Support within 14 days of delivery.

Customers should provide the order number and photographs showing the damage.

For confirmed damaged-product claims, NovaRetail may issue a replacement or refund depending on inventory availability and customer preference.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are normally processed within 5-7 business days.

The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's bank account may vary depending on the payment provider.

## Non-Refundable Items

Customized products and digital products are generally non-refundable unless the product is defective or NovaRetail explicitly approves an exception.
